# Backlog verification B

Research round r2, compiled 2026-11-07. Each finding shows its confidence and source type. Fact-checker verdicts are listed first. **A "corrected" or "unsupported" verdict overrides the finding.**

## Fact-check

Of the 8 claims, 3 are confirmed: the Pakistan RCT published numbers, Epoch's 47%/qtr, 13x/yr, 75x to 4.7x/yr, and ARC-AGI-1 at 87.5% falling from $4,560 to $0.30 per task. That last one comes with caveats: the o3-preview cost is a retroactive estimate at o3-pro pricing for an unreleased model, and Grok 4.6 may not be the cheapest entry at or above 87.5%. Two are corrected. Gemini 4 Argon's 'doubling' is the end of an introductory price that Google announced at launch, and Google gives no reason. The NHC official report exists and says GDMI only 'slightly outperformed' official Atlantic track forecasts at 12-72 h; the 66.5 vs 81.4 n mi figures were not found. Three are unsupported. The IMO 'officially graded' claim rests on Xiaohongshu's statement, with no IMO statement, and the other labs' 42/42 were self-run or informal. ARC-AGI-2 '~90% at $0.44/task' has no primary source, though Deep Think's 84.6% at $13.62 checks out. The Artificial Analysis '21 to 58 at 11x' cannot be found, and the index went through about 10 version changes in that year. Advice: drop or rewrite the unsupported items, and build the 'same capability, cheaper' chart from ARC Prize verified cost-per-task data or Epoch's frontier method, not mixed sources.

- **confirmed**: Pakistan RCT (Nature Health, 2026): LLM alone beat LLM-assisted physicians by 11.5 pp (95% CI 5.5-17.5).  
  → Qazi, Ali, Khawaja, Akhtar, Sheikh, Alizai et al., 'Large language model diagnostic assistance for physicians in a lower-middle-income country: a randomized controlled trial', Nature Health 1, 198-205, published 6 Feb 2026, DOI 10.1038/s44360-025-00007-8. 60 physicians randomized, 58 completed (29 per arm), 342 cases. The model was GPT-4o. Primary outcome: 71.4% (SD 21.8) with LLM vs 42.6% (SD 22.7) with conventional resources; adjusted difference 27.5 pp (95% CI 22.8-32.2; P<0.001). Time per case: -6.4 s (95% CI -68.2 to 55.3; P=0.84), so no difference. Exploratory comparison: LLM alone 82.9% (SD 10.4), 11.5 pp higher than LLM-assisted physicians (95% CI 5.5-17.5; P<0.001). In 31.4% of cases the physician+LLM group beat the median LLM-alone score.  
  Source: https://www.nature.com/articles/s44360-025-00007-8

- **confirmed**: Epoch 'The plunging price of thought' (Sep 22, 2026): 47%/quarter, ~13x/yr; ~75x/yr at SOTA debut slowing to ~4.7x/yr after two years.  
  → Emberson & Roodman, Epoch AI, published 22 Sep 2026, updated 23 Sep. The cost to reach a given performance level falls about 47% per quarter (~13x/yr), averaged over five benchmarks since 2023. When a level is first state of the art, the decline is 66%/quarter (~75x/yr). Two years later it is 32%/quarter (~4.7x/yr). By domain: game puzzles 39-43%/qtr, math 50-52%/qtr, GPQA Diamond 47.0%/qtr. Changing the averaging grid for GPQA gives 42.9-58.0%/qtr.  
  Source: https://epoch.ai/publications/the-plunging-price-of-thought  
  Method: the five primary benchmarks are AIME (OTIS Mock), Chess Puzzles, FrontierMath T1-3, GPQA Diamond and Mystery Game Puzzles. Price means the actual dollar cost of the benchmark run per question, not list price per token; rented-hardware cost is used for open models without an API. Lower token budgets are simulated with the CAISI method. The series follows the cheapest model on the cost-accuracy Pareto frontier at each date, fitted with Pareto logistic or least-squares models. No standard errors are given ('reasonable but rough'). Caveats from the authors: training to the benchmark, benchmarks are a weak proxy for useful work, users do not switch to the cheapest model, only about 3 years of data, and model sampling is ad hoc. The method matches the user's 'same capability, cheaper' framing.
