## Fact-check

All seven claims hold up in substance, but two need correcting. (1) Big-four 2026 capex is about $720-740B (midpoint about $730B), not $720-750B. The bases are mixed, and 2027 'higher' is directional only: Amazon says 'meaningfully higher', Alphabet's >$300B is single-source, and Microsoft and Meta gave no numbers. (3) Huang's 'AGI has arrived' post (X, Sep 6, 2026) congratulated OpenAI on Astra but was not tied to the 99.9% ARC-AGI-3 score. That score is OpenAI's own harness; ARC Prize's standard harness gave 62.7%. Brockman's quote, the OpenAI research-intern metrics, the Opus 5.5 CoBench numbers, the RLI 21.25% vs 2.5%, and the GDPval 84.9% and 47.6% are confirmed. Several rest on secondary coverage because the primary pages returned 403 or were too large. The vendor-reported figures (OpenAI intern metrics, GDPval, ARC 99.9%) should be labelled as vendor-reported.
