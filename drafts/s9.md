## What to watch

Nine dated checks: what would show the overtake spreading or stalling, and where to look. Last checked: 7 November 2026.

<!-- CHART: s9-signposts | Signpost list: date, indicator, value as of 7 Nov 2026, "spreading" and "stalling" thresholds, link to source, and a status chip (not yet due / moved / no change); a decay banner above it shows "data as of 7 Nov 2026" and the days elapsed | none (values inline, from footnotes s9-*) -->

1. **4 December 2026, ARC Prize results.** Best verified ARC-AGI-3 score on the standard setup: 62.7%, from a run over the $10,000 cost cap.[^s9-arc] Spreading: higher scores within the cap. Stalling: gains only on company-built setups. Check arcprize.org/leaderboard, also for an ARC-AGI-4 date.
2. **31 December 2026, forecasts resolve.** Forecasters expected the 80% time horizon to reach 3.4 hours; the last measurement was about 3.1.[^s9-leap] If AI falls short, it is a rare forecast AI did not beat. Check forecastingresearch.org.
3. **1 January 2027, Epoch's public index.** Epoch projected its frontier at about 170 (166 to 176).[^s9-eci] Above 176: public evidence of a second speed-up. Still at 169: none. Check epoch.ai/data/eci_scores.csv.
4. **7 May 2027, the price of GPT-5-level AI.** Now $0.20 per million tokens.[^s9-price] Spreading: lower still. Stalling: rivals copying Google's 1 December price doubling for Gemini 4 Argon.[^s9-argon] Check the labs' price pages.
5. **Monthly, the Remote Labor Index.** The best agent does about 21% of real freelance projects to human standard.[^s9-rli] Above one in three: the work gap is closing. Flat into mid-2027: a stall. Check labs.scale.com/leaderboard/rli.
6. **By 7 May 2027, a new METR ruler.** No model newer than April 2026 has a reliable public time horizon.[^s9-metr] A successor would let outsiders test the private acceleration finding. Check metr.org/time-horizons.
7. **Any day, a threshold declared.** Anthropic told investors it "may imminently meet" its threshold for automated AI research, reworded three times this year.[^s9-s1] Google says Gemini 4 Argon is below its own.[^s9-google] Watch for a declaration, or another rewording.
8. **November 2027, METR's bottleneck call.** METR saw no convincing bottlenecks for 12 months.[^s9-accel] A follow-up, or a second lab opening its data, would test the 1.9x speed-up.
9. **When published, real-job studies.** METR's redesigned developer trial; updated Danish payroll data (section 1).[^s9-work] Clear gains would mean the overtake has reached paid work.

Today "already past us" holds for tasks with an answer key, not for messy paid work. If the work measures start moving with the test scores, the overtake is spreading. If only the test scores move, the title stays true where it is true now, and no further.
