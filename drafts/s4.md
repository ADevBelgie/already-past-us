## 4. The jagged map

Put every head-to-head test of AI against people on one line, running from "far below humans" to "beyond the best humans", and the line looks like a saw blade. The chart colours each dot by who checked the result.

**Beyond the best humans.** The opening's two results sit at the far right: the 2025 programming world finals (OpenAI's own report) and the Erdős disproof (section 0). In a Nature paper, Google DeepMind's GenCast weather model beat Europe's leading physics-based forecast ensemble on 97.2% of 1,320 test targets.[^s4-gencast] Hurricanes show AI beating an expert team's official product. The US National Hurricane Center's own review of the 2025 Atlantic season found DeepMind's model "slightly outperformed" the official track forecasts from 12 to 72 hours ahead.[^s4-nhc]

**Ahead of trained people, with caveats.** On GPQA, graduate-level science questions written so that web searches don't help, PhD experts answering in their own field scored about 65%.[^s4-gpqa-human] In Epoch AI's testing, top models score about 87–90% on its hardest subset.[^s4-gpqa-ai] In a randomised trial in Pakistan, GPT-4o working alone scored 82.9% on diagnostic cases. Doctors using GPT-4o scored 71.4%. That gap comes from an exploratory secondary analysis, not the trial's main question.[^s4-pakistan] Waymo says its driverless cars had 95% fewer serious-injury crashes than human drivers over 270 million miles. That is Waymo's own analysis, against average drivers.[^s4-waymo]

**Claimed, not confirmed.** At the 2026 International Mathematical Olympiad, Huawei and Xiaohongshu reported perfect scores of 42 out of 42 and said the organisers graded them. The IMO has published no statement. Seven human contestants also got full marks, so even at face value this is a tie with the best.[^s4-imo]

**Level with the best.** In July 2026 the Forecasting Research Institute found the best AI forecasters statistically indistinguishable from superforecasters on its questions about future events.[^s4-forecast]

**Below humans.** The best agent completes only about 21% of real freelance projects on the Remote Labor Index (section 0).[^s4-rli] ARC-AGI-3, a set of puzzle games that people can solve every one of, launched in March 2026 with every frontier model under 1%.[^s4-arc-launch] By September GPT-6 Astra reached 62.7% on ARC Prize's standard setup, verified by ARC Prize.[^s4-arc-astra] Fast, but still well short of people.

**Far below humans.** Epoch AI's February 2026 review found robots typically 3 to 10 times slower than people. No robot could finish every part of cleaning a kitchen, or pull a trash bag out of an indoor bin.[^s4-robots]

The pattern has a shape. The dots on the right share an answer key and a short, self-contained task. The dots on the left involve long, open-ended work or the physical world. Asking whether AI has reached "human level" flattens this saw blade into a single number, which is why section 5 sets that question aside.

> **Expert note:** Several right-hand dots compare AI with machines or with average people, not with the best humans. GenCast was measured against ECMWF's physics ensemble (ENS), and ECMWF noted the comparison used an older, coarser version of it.[^s4-gencast] Waymo's benchmark is the average human driver in the same places.[^s4-waymo] In the Pakistan trial, the primary result is that doctors with GPT-4o beat doctors with conventional resources by 27.5 points, after 20 hours of AI-literacy training. In 31.4% of cases the doctor-plus-AI group beat the median AI-alone score.[^s4-pakistan] We left Microsoft's MAI-DxO result off the map (85.5% of 304 hard NEJM cases vs 20% for physicians). Those physicians were barred from books, colleagues and the internet, and the paper is a vendor-authored preprint.[^s4-maidxo] For hurricanes, the NHC report also finds DeepMind's model beat the official forecast in the East Pacific at 48–120 hours. We have not read the report's error tables ourselves.[^s4-nhc]

> **Expert note:** The GPQA comparison crosses question sets. The 65% expert figure is on the 448-question main set. The model scores are on the 198-question Diamond subset, whose expert score is inflated by how its questions were selected. GPQA is multiple-choice, near saturation and possibly contaminated, so beating PhDs on it is not the same as doing a PhD's work.[^s4-gpqa-human][^s4-gpqa-ai] The forecasting parity rests on a superforecaster baseline collected in 2024 and extrapolated, and the confidence intervals overlap.[^s4-forecast] On ARC-AGI-3, Astra scored 99.9% with a harness OpenAI supplied. Only the standard-harness score is comparable across companies.[^s4-arc-astra] At the IMO, four more labs' models reportedly scored 42/42 in an informal test run by an investor, graded by AI agents. Axiom's Lean-checked run took about 1,496 minutes in total, far beyond the human limit of two 4.5-hour sessions.[^s4-imo]

[^s4-gencast]: **Verified.** Price et al., "Probabilistic weather forecasting with machine learning" (GenCast), Nature, December 2024, doi:10.1038/s41586-024-08252-9. GenCast was more skilful than ECMWF's ENS ensemble on 97.2% of 1,320 verification targets, and on 99.8% at lead times beyond 36 hours (Nature figures; the arXiv preprint says 97.4%). The comparison is with a physics-based model, not human forecasters. https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.15796

[^s4-nhc]: **Verified.** Cangialosi, Martinez, Reinhart and Papin, National Hurricane Center, "Forecast Verification Report: 2025 Hurricane Season", 30 March 2026. In a like-for-like (homogeneous) comparison, Google DeepMind's cyclone model (GDMI) "slightly outperformed" the official Atlantic track forecast at 12–72 hours, with comparable intensity skill. In the East Pacific it beat the official forecast and all consensus aids at 48–120 hours. These findings were read through secondary coverage (WIS-TV, 6 April 2026), because the PDF was too large to fetch. https://www.nhc.noaa.gov/verification/pdfs/Verification_2025.pdf

[^s4-gpqa-human]: **Verified.** Rein et al., "GPQA: A Graduate-Level Google-Proof Q&A Benchmark", arXiv 2311.12022, November 2023. On the 448-question main set, in-domain PhD experts scored 65% (74% after discounting clear mistakes), and skilled non-experts with web access scored 34%. https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.12022

[^s4-gpqa-ai]: **Verified.** Epoch AI benchmarking hub, GPQA Diamond (198 questions). Epoch-run scores for top models are about 87–90%, for example Grok 4 at 87% (July 2025). Figures of up to 96% for 2026 models come from aggregators and vendors and are not used here. https://epoch.ai/benchmarks

[^s4-pakistan]: **Verified.** Qazi et al., "Large language model diagnostic assistance for physicians in a lower-middle-income country: a randomized controlled trial", Nature Health 1, 198–205, 6 February 2026. 58 physicians completed the trial (29 per arm) across 342 cases, using GPT-4o, after 20 hours of AI-literacy training. Primary outcome: 71.4% with the LLM vs 42.6% with conventional resources (adjusted difference 27.5 points, 95% CI 22.8–32.2). Exploratory secondary analysis: LLM alone 82.9%, which is 11.5 points above LLM-assisted physicians (95% CI 5.5–17.5). In 31.4% of cases the physician-plus-LLM group beat the median LLM-alone score. https://www.nature.com/articles/s44360-025-00007-8

[^s4-waymo]: **Vendor-reported.** Waymo, "Safety data update, September 2026", 24 September 2026. Over 270 million rider-only miles through June 2026: 95% fewer serious-injury-or-worse crashes and 82% fewer injury crashes than Waymo's own location-matched human-driver benchmark. No confidence intervals are given. https://waymo.com/blog/shorts/safetydata-september26/

[^s4-imo]: **Vendor-reported.** AFP via France 24, "AI catches up with humans to score 100% at top maths contest", 23 July 2026. Huawei's Celia and Xiaohongshu's dots-note-3.0 reported 42/42 at IMO 2026 (Shanghai, 15–16 July). Only Xiaohongshu says it submitted its solutions to the IMO organisers for grading, and imo-official.org carries no statement on AI grading. AFP reports that 7 of 666 human contestants got full marks, citing the official results table; the IMO's own posts speak of nearly 700 contestants. Investor Deedy Das reported 42/42 for models from OpenAI, Anthropic, Axiom and Moonshot in his own unofficial test. Axiom's repository (github.com/AxiomMath/IMO2026) claims Lean-checked proofs, with per-problem times totalling about 1,496 minutes. https://www.france24.com/en/live-news/20260723-ai-catches-up-with-humans-to-score-100-at-top-maths-contest

[^s4-forecast]: **Verified.** Forecasting Research Institute, "AI models have likely reached parity with superforecasters on ForecastBench", 16 July 2026. The top system (Cassi) could not be distinguished statistically from superforecasters (one-sided bootstrap p = 0.41; xAI 0.15–0.16, Google DeepMind 0.14). It is the first AI to rank above the superforecaster median on market questions. The superforecaster baseline was collected in 2024 and is extrapolated. https://forecastingresearch.substack.com/p/ai-models-have-likely-reached-parity

[^s4-rli]: **Verified.** Scale AI and Center for AI Safety, Remote Labor Index leaderboard, accessed 7 November 2026: Claude Opus 5.5 21.25% and GPT-6 Astra 20.83%, a statistical tie, across 240 paid freelance projects. https://labs.scale.com/leaderboard/rli

[^s4-arc-launch]: **Verified.** ARC Prize, ARC-AGI-3 technical report, arXiv 2603.24621, March 2026 (launched 25 March 2026). Frontier systems scored below 1%, and humans solve 100% of the environments. The often-cited best launch score of 0.37% (Gemini 3.1 Pro) comes from the leaderboard and press. https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.24621

[^s4-arc-astra]: **Verified.** ARC Prize (Greg Kamradt), "OpenAI's GPT-6 Astra on ARC-AGI-3", 3 September 2026 (leaderboard entry 2 September 2026). GPT-6 Astra scored 62.7% at max effort on the Standard harness, at about $26,098, and 99.9% at high effort on OpenAI's Provider Adapter harness. ARC Prize: "we are not claiming that it is AGI". Leaderboard as of early November 2026: GPT-6.1 Sol 52.7%, Gemini 4 Argon 49.8% (Standard). https://arcprize.org/blog/astra ; https://arcprize.org/leaderboard

[^s4-robots]: **Verified.** Epoch AI, "Where Autonomy Works: Evaluating Robot Capabilities in 2026", 10 February 2026. Robots are typically 3–10 times slower than humans (2–10 times on household tasks). No robot completed all kitchen-cleaning subtasks or removed a trash bag from an indoor bin, and reliable use is limited to narrow warehouse and inspection settings. https://epoch.ai/publications/where-autonomy-works-evaluating-robot-capabilities-in-2026

[^s4-maidxo]: **Vendor-reported.** Nori et al. (Microsoft AI), "Sequential Diagnosis with Language Models", arXiv 2506.22405, June–July 2025 (preprint). MAI-DxO with OpenAI o3 diagnosed 85.5% of 304 NEJM case records correctly, vs 20% for 21 practising physicians who were barred from textbooks, colleagues, the internet and AI. https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.22405
