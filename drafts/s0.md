## Already past us, in places

<!-- CHART: s0-cards | Three stat cards side by side: ICPC 2025 (AI 12/12 vs best human team 11), Erdős 1946 conjecture disproved (checked by nine mathematicians), best AI agent ~21% of real freelance projects; each card shows its counter-fact and a provenance tag linking to the footnote | none (values inline, from footnotes s0-icpc, s0-erdos, s0-rli) -->

At the 2025 world finals of the top university programming contest (ICPC), an OpenAI system solved all 12 problems. The best human team solved 11.[^s0-icpc] The counter-fact: that is OpenAI's own report, and at the 2025 International Olympiad in Informatics, five human contestants still beat OpenAI's entry.[^s0-ioi]

In May 2026 an OpenAI model disproved Paul Erdős's 1946 "unit distance" conjecture in geometry. Nine mathematicians, including Noga Alon and Tim Gowers, checked it and published a simplified version.[^s0-erdos] The counter-fact: humans improved on the AI's construction within weeks, and in October 2025 OpenAI had wrongly claimed GPT-5 solved ten Erdős problems it had only found in existing papers.[^s0-erdos-context]

Yet on the Remote Labor Index, built from 240 real paid freelance projects, the best AI agent produces work judged as good as a human freelancer's on only about 21% of them. Claude Opus 5.5 and GPT-6 Astra are tied at the top, as of 7 November 2026.[^s0-rli] The counter-fact runs the other way: at launch in October 2025 the best score was 2.5%, about eight times lower.[^s0-rli-growth]

Side by side, these facts don't fit on one line. AI is past the best humans at some narrow tasks with an answer key, and still fails about four in five real freelance jobs.[^s0-rli] So "has AI reached human level?" is the wrong question; the answer depends on the task. This briefing goes task by task: how much cheaper the same ability got (about 17 times in a year),[^s0-price] how long a task AI can finish on its own, where it leads and where it lags, what the "AGI" claims mean, and early, hard-to-check evidence that AI is speeding up AI research.[^s0-accel] Every number carries a tag saying who measured it.

> **Expert note:** The ICPC result ran in a separate experimental track. ICPC confirmed the AI's solutions were accepted, but its review did not validate the system or the underlying model. Google DeepMind's Gemini 2.5 Deep Think solved 10 of 12 in the same track, which would have ranked 2nd of 139 university teams, and it solved one problem (Problem C) that no university team solved (DeepMind-reported). On the Remote Labor Index the top two scores (21.25% and 20.83%) are one project apart out of 240 and the leaderboard publishes no confidence intervals, so neither model should be called the leader. Scale AI, which co-runs the index, is a commercial data vendor.

[^s0-ioi]: **Vendor-reported.** OpenAI researchers' posts, relayed by The Decoder, "OpenAI's AI system wins a gold medal-level score at the International Olympiad in Informatics 2025", August 2025: in the online AI track, OpenAI's ensemble of general reasoning models placed above all but 5 of 330 human contestants (about the 98th percentile) under the same 5-hour, 50-submission rules. The IOI did not certify the result. https://the-decoder.com/openais-ai-system-wins-a-gold-medal-level-score-at-the-international-olympiad-in-informatics-2025/

[^s0-erdos]: **Verified.** Alon, Bloom, Gowers, Litt, Sawin, Shankar, Tsimerman, Wang and Wood, "Remarks on the disproof of the unit distance conjecture", arXiv:2605.20695, 20 May 2026 (a preprint; its abstract refers to "the recent OpenAI-generated counterexample"), https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.20695. Endorsement by Noga Alon, Melanie Wood and Thomas Bloom reported in TechCrunch, "OpenAI claims it solved an 80-year-old math problem — for real this time", 20 May 2026, https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/20/openai-claims-it-solved-an-80-year-old-math-problem-for-real-this-time/. [^s0-erdos-context]: **Verified.** Human improvement on the AI result within weeks: Quanta Magazine, "Why the Legendary Erdős Problems Are Falling to AI", 3 August 2026, https://www.quantamagazine.org/why-the-legendary-erdos-problems-are-falling-to-ai-20260803/. The October 2025 episode (OpenAI's Kevin Weil said GPT-5 had solved 10 Erdős problems; it had found existing literature, and Thomas Bloom called the claim a "dramatic misrepresentation"): TechCrunch, 20 May 2026, https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/20/openai-claims-it-solved-an-80-year-old-math-problem-for-real-this-time/

[^s0-rli]: **Verified.** Scale AI and Center for AI Safety, Remote Labor Index leaderboard, accessed 7 November 2026 (the page has no update date and reports no confidence intervals): Claude Opus 5.5 21.25%, GPT-6 Astra 20.83%, Fable 5.1 17.92%, Gemini Argon 4 17.08%. The automation rate is the share of the 240 projects (which had paid human freelancers $143,991 in total) where the AI's deliverable was judged at least as good as the human one. "Four in five" fail: 100% minus 21.25% = 78.75%. https://labs.scale.com/leaderboard/rli

[^s0-rli-growth]: **Our calculation.** From the Remote Labor Index: best score at launch 2.50% (Manus 1.0; launch paper arXiv 2510.26787, 30 October 2025) and best score 21.25% on the leaderboard accessed 7 November 2026; 21.25 / 2.50 = 8.5. https://labs.scale.com/leaderboard/rli

[^s0-price]: **Our calculation.** From the Epoch Capabilities Index (eci_scores.csv, downloaded 7 November 2026) and official list prices, blended 3:1 input to output. The cheapest model at GPT-5 level (ECI 150 or above) cost $3.44 per million tokens (GPT-5, November 2025) and costs $0.20 now (GPT-6 Luna or Claude Haiku 5.5, November 2026), about 17 times less. All snapshots use today's list prices; historical prices are still being added. Per-token prices ignore how many tokens a model uses. https://epoch.ai/data/eci_scores.csv

[^s0-accel]: **Verified.** METR, "Independent Assessment of AI R&D Acceleration at Anthropic in October 2026", 5 November 2026. Anthropic redacted a majority of the evidence METR considered most informative. https://metr.org/blog/2026-11-05-anthropic-ai-rd-acceleration-assessment/
